Privacy & activity suggestions

Questions and up to six recent answers stay in this page’s memory, not on our server. New conversation clears them. The public guide cannot read private guest records, messages or payments.

Suggestions appear only while browsing, at most three per visit. Timed reminders follow published times, not confirmed progress. No SMS, email or background notifications. Only these preferences and seen reminder IDs are saved on this device.

A separate authenticated feed watches recent guest activity while you are signed in as admin. Saved connections choose Sam or Jossy; ambiguous connections are skipped. Legacy submissions matched by unique name are labelled, not treated as verified ownership. Alerts never enter the public guide or its conversation.
